# Alex — Traditional Anime Art

[View my portfolio](https://alex72-py.github.io/)

I draw anime fanart by hand using ink and Ohuhu alcohol markers. My portfolio includes selected drawings, current commission availability, and contact details.

For commission enquiries, please read my [commission terms](https://alex72-py.github.io/terms/) and contact me through the portfolio. I work around a flexible schedule and discuss timing before starting.
